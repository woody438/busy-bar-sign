Outside these hours, while the sign would say FREE or that you're not here, the wall goes black — an OLED's pixels off — and so does the screen saver; a call, a meeting or a warning lights it as usual. The Out of hours button above shows it.

Only your own events — ones with no invitees — are matched by title, and all-day and Out of Office ones for OUT ON SITE, as whole words in any case. Meetings are sorted by their location, invitees and join link, so their titles never matter.

Where meetings are

YOUR OFFICE
MEETING ROOMS
COMPANY SITES

A meeting’s location says where you’ll be; a Teams or Zoom link only that others can dial in. Your office, a meeting room — a room booked on the invite, a location with one of these words, or a code like MTL-210 — or one of your company’s sites — a location with one of the Company sites words, such as your company’s name — is MEETING, even with a link (a room, and LATE FOR MTG if you’re still at the Mac). Anywhere else is OUT ON SITE. No location, or one like “TBC” or a dial-in, goes by the link: a call, else MEETING.

What you're looking at

The wall display rebuilt as a BUSY Bar, on the same call detection as the original. Every LED is drawn by the same engine the Mac app runs — the Swift port is checked against this page LED by LED. The pixel fonts, colours, dot shape and transitions all come from the BUSY Bar's own open-source firmware. The clock is your real time; the call state here is a demo — or press Calendar day to play a scripted day through the calendar rules.

Why 118 × 16

Sixteen rows is what gives the type its chunk, so that stays. A real bar is 72 columns — too narrow to set "ON A CALL" beside a clock — so the panel runs wider. At 32 pixels per LED it lands exactly on a 4K screen, full width and 31% tall.

The status pill

A glossy pill in the firmware's exact reds and greens, with a slow sheen crossing it. The mic echoes the firmware's own on-call theme; the tick is the "available" mark every meeting app uses. It says "ON A CALL", not "ON CALL", which in British English means on standby.

A change of state

As on the device: the old screen presses down, a ring bursts from the top edge and floods the panel, and the new screen springs up. The status fills the whole bar in 14-row capitals for a few seconds, then the pill draws back and the time slides in beside it.

The clock

Set as the firmware's clock app sets it: bold 7-row digits in white on black, the colon dimming on odd seconds, and the day beneath at half brightness.

LEDs and glass

Dots follow Flipper's own preview shader — rounded squares at 85% of the pitch, shading toward their corners — and unlit LEDs vanish into the glossy black face. Colours are corrected for the firmware's LED gamma, so dim tones sit back the way they do on the device.

Small screens

The Stacked layout button shows the option for small monitors such as 960 × 540: the pill across the top, the time large beneath it, the LEDs filling the screen. The announcement fills the whole panel on two lines, then draws up into the pill as the clock rises.

Looking after the screen

A wall display shows the same thing for hours, which an OLED remembers. So the LEDs never stand still: they move a pixel at a time through every position within an LED, so no pixel is always lit and no gap always dark. After half an hour of FREE they ease down to about 60% of their light, and every 15 minutes the word scrolls out and back. Out of hours, with nothing going on, the wall goes black. Quiet hour shows the first three, sped up; Out of hours the last.

Do Not Disturb

Double-click the display for 30 minutes of DO NOT DISTURB: an indigo pill with a moon, and a countdown where the clock was. Double-click again to end it. In the app a call still shows ON A CALL, then Do Not Disturb returns with whatever time is left.

Your calendar

Press Calendar day to play a working day through the rules: 10 minutes before a call the bar turns amber with a countdown; if the call starts without you it pulses LATE FOR CALL, and never joined, it says MEETING; leave early and it says FREE TILL the slot ends if something follows straight after, or just FREE if nothing does. On a call or in a meeting the clock counts down to when you're free, through back-to-back meetings. A meeting's location says where you'll be: your office or a meeting room is MEETING, even with a Teams link, and anywhere else is OUT ON SITE. Tentative meetings say CALL TBC. Lunch, your own blocks and flights show LUNCH, AWAY and OUT OF OFFICE.

Who wins

Your own controls first, then the microphone, then the away keys (Nearby, Lunch, Back In), then Do Not Disturb, then the calendar: out of office, lunch, away, a phone call, an in-person meeting, a call you're late for, a warning, a tentative meeting; then NEARBY if the Mac's locked; then a no-meetings block, and a call you left early with something straight after. The microphone always turns it red.

Same detection underneath

Unchanged from the original: CoreAudio says which app has the microphone, dictation apps are ignored, and changes are debounced so the bar never flickers. A menu-bar item can force either state.
