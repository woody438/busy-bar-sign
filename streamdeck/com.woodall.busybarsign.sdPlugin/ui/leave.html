A tap leaves the meeting, when you let go. Hold the key a second to end a Zoom meeting you're hosting for everyone; in any other meeting, holding just leaves.

A tap only shows HOLD, so a stray press can't leave your meeting. Hold the key half a second, then let go, to leave; hold it 1.6 seconds to end a Zoom meeting you're hosting for everyone.

Teams and Zoom are pressed by the Busy Bar Sign app, which needs Accessibility (its Meetings settings).
